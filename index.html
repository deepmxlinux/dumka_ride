    Questions

    How do I book a cab in Dumka?

    Fill in pickup, destination, date and time, then send the prepared request on WhatsApp. You get a confirmation message or a call back.

    How is the fare decided?

    The fare is based on road distance. The page shows a range before you send. Tolls and parking are extra and the final fare is confirmed on WhatsApp.

    Can you take me to the airport or railway station?

    Yes. Rides to Deoghar airport, Jasidih junction and Bhagalpur are common, so choose the pickup time with the train or flight in mind.

    Do you go outside Jharkhand?

    Trips to Tarapith and Kolkata in West Bengal are available. For other cities, message the route for a fare.
